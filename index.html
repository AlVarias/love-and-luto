The Story Behind the Food

Hi, I'm Allen! I'll let you in on a little secret: I'm not a professionally trained chef. By day, my real profession is a Systems Engineer.

I moved here to New Zealand for my amazing partner. My culinary journey didn't start in a culinary school; it started in our kitchen, simply trying to cook the authentic Filipino dishes she missed and loved. Everything you see on this menu started as a meal made with love for my soon-to-be fiancée (and soon-to-be wife!).

Now, I pour that exact same love, care, and dedication into every single batch I make for you. From kneading dough to slow-simmering broths, it's all about sharing a taste of home.
